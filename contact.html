Contact

Manch March is made by Pankaj Bhagat, a computer science student and independent game developer from Ludhiana, Punjab, India.

Bug reports, suggestions and feedback

The best place to reach the developer is GitHub: github.com/bhagatpankaj1313-ui. Open an issue on the Manch March repository, or send a message through GitHub.

When reporting a bug, it helps to include: your device (phone or computer), your browser, the level or mode where it happened, and what you expected to happen instead.

Privacy questions

Questions about the Privacy Policy can also be sent through GitHub using the link above.
