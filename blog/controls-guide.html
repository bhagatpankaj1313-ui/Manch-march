Controls guide

On a phone or tablet

  • On-screen joystick to move the march leader.
  • Aim with your thumb; the fire button follows your aim thumb.
  • On-screen buttons to fire and to use powers.
  • Play in landscape for the full view — the game is built for it.

On a computer

  • Keyboard to move, mouse to aim and shoot.
  • Number keys or on-screen buttons for powers.
  • The pause menu covers the exact controls for the mode you are playing.

Making controls feel right

If movement or aiming ever feels off, open Settings from the title screen and adjust sensitivity. The in-game coach also walks through each control the first time it appears.
