What is Manch March?

Manch March is a 2D top-down stealth-action game. You lead a march toward the fictional Kranti Maidan while police lines try to shut it down. It is free, needs no account, and runs in a browser or as a small Android app.

The twist: your allies are your power. Every ally in the chain adds strength. Lose the whole chain and the march is busted. So every level is two games at once — push forward, and keep the march together.

What is in it

  • 60 campaign levels across five chapters, with a boss every 10th stage.
  • Police mode: switch sides and hold the ground against endless waves.
  • Online police co-op for up to 4 officers, joined by room code.
  • Boss rush, a villain campaign, daily marches, a shop and upgrades.
  • A non-lethal, cartoon tone: knockouts, gas and dispersals, never killing.
