How the ally chain system works

The ally chain is the whole game in one rule: each ally adds one point of power, and zero allies means the march is busted.

That one rule shapes every decision. A big march pushes through police lines; a thin march cannot. Fighting wins ground, but every ally lost to a patrol, a gas cloud or a bad charge weakens everything that comes after.

Three habits that win levels

  1. Gather before you fight. Pick up allies along the route before hitting a hard line. A stronger chain survives contact.
  2. Protect the middle, not just yourself. Barricades, walls and powers work best when they shield the chain, not one character.
  3. Spend powers where they change the outcome. Speech, Kite, Umbrella and Dog are limited each match. Use them on the line that would otherwise break you, not the first patrol you see.

Police mode mirrors the rule from the other side: there, breaking the other side's chain is your job, and Netaji's health is your own fail state.
