Frequently Asked Questions

Is Manch March free?

Yes. The web version is free to play here, and the Android APK is free to download. There are no accounts and no forced purchases; coins and upgrades are earned by playing.

Where do I download it?

On the Download page. The APK is about 1.8 MB and needs Android 7.0 or newer.

Why does my phone ask about "unknown sources"?

The game is not on the Play Store yet, so Android asks you to allow installs from your browser for this one install. That permission can stay limited to the browser you downloaded from.

Does it need the internet?

Campaign, police mode, boss rush and the villain campaign all work offline. Online police co-op needs an internet connection and a room code from the host.

How do I unlock levels?

Clear the previous level. Level 1 is open from the start; each cleared stage opens the next one. Your stars show how well you cleared each stage.

How does police co-op work?

One player hosts and shares a room code; up to 4 officers join the same match. The host runs the match, everyone holds the same ground, and co-op protesters are tougher than solo ones.

Does my progress save?

Yes, on your own device. On the web, progress lives in your browser's local storage; in the APK, it lives in the app's storage. Clearing browser data or app data resets progress.

Who made Manch March?

Pankaj Bhagat, a computer science student and independent developer from Ludhiana, Punjab, India. See the About page.

Is it based on real events?

No. The story, characters, places (including Kranti Maidan) and artwork are fictional and created for the game, with a non-lethal tone throughout.
