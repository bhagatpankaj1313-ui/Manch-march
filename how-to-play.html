How to Play

Manch March is easy to pick up and hard to master. This page covers the goal, the ally chain, and the controls.

▶ Play Now

The goal

Lead your march to Kranti Maidan, the fictional rally ground at the heart of the game. Each level throws police lines, patrols, barricades and hazards across your route. Reach the Maidan with your march intact to clear the level and move on to the next.

The ally chain

Your march is your strength. Every ally walking with you adds one point of power to the march. Police action can break allies away from the chain — if the chain ever falls to zero allies, the march is busted and the run ends. Gathering new allies along the route is just as important as fighting: a big march is a strong march.

Police pressure

Expect patrol officers, gas, barricades and roadblocks, and on some levels the heavy VAJRA boss vehicle. You can fight back with the weapons you pick up, but charging in alone is a fast way to lose allies. Use cover, timing and the march's powers to break through lines with the fewest losses.

Powers

  • Speech 📢 — rally the march and strengthen nearby allies.
  • Kite 🪁 — a scouting and distraction aid from above.
  • Umbrella ☂️ — cover against what rains down on the march.
  • Dog 🐕 — a loyal companion that helps the march on its way.

Powers are limited each match, so spend them where they change the outcome, not on the first patrol you see.

Build and defend

The Engineer can place barricades and walls to shape the battlefield — block a road, protect a flank, or buy the march time to regroup. Placed defences work best when they protect the ally chain, not just your own position.

Controls

  • Phone / tablet: on-screen joystick to move, aim with your thumb, and on-screen buttons to fire and use powers. Play in landscape for the full view.
  • Computer: keyboard to move, mouse to aim and shoot, number keys or on-screen buttons for powers.

In-game buttons and the pause menu explain the exact controls for the mode you are playing. If a control ever feels off, the settings menu lets you adjust sensitivity.

Modes

  • Campaign — level-by-level progress toward the Maidan.
  • Protesters mode — lead the march against the police lines.
  • Police co-op — online mode where a friend joins as a second officer (needs an internet connection and a room code).
