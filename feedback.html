Feedback

Found a bug, have an idea, or just want to say what you think? That helps more than you know — this is a one-person game.

Report a bug or suggest a feature

Open an issue on the game's GitHub repository: github.com/bhagatpankaj1313-ui/manch-march/issues

It helps to include: your device (phone model or computer), your browser if you play on the web, the level or mode where it happened, and what you expected to happen instead. A screenshot or short screen recording is even better.
